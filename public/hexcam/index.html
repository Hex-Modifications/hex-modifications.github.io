    /ABOUT

    hexcam turns clean phone footage into the look of mid-2000s pocket cameras, flip phones, webcams, camcorders and film: low resolution, highlights glowing violet, blocky JPEG artefacts, noise and the burned-in orange date. Every effect works on video as well as photos, and video exports keep their sound.

    Video adds what stills can't: dropped frame rates, handheld shake, tape tracking errors, chroma bleed, interlacing, ghosting, flicker, moving light leaks, animated grain and a date stamp whose clock ticks. Exports record in real time to MP4, or WebM in browsers that can't record MP4.

    Nothing is uploaded. There's no account and no tracking. Photos and videos are read, edited and saved on your device.

    Which browsers work?
    Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, Android and iOS.
    Why does export take as long as the video?
    It records while the clip plays at normal speed, so the sound stays in sync.
    Can I share a look?
    Copy look link puts every setting in the URL.
    What is bleach bypass?
    The film-lab trick of skipping the bleach stage: more contrast, less colour, silvery highlights.

    hexcam is a free tool by Hex Modifications.
