what it does

hexcam turns clean phone footage into the look of mid-2000s pocket cameras, flip phones, webcams, camcorders and film: the low resolution, the blown highlights glowing violet, the blocky JPEG artefacts, the noise and the burned-in orange date. Unlike most of these editors, every effect also works on video, with the sound kept.

how it works

Everything runs on your GPU with WebGL2. Compression isn't faked with a texture: the image is converted to YCbCr, chroma is subsampled (4:2:0, 4:2:2 or the 4:1:1 of DV tape), every 8×8 block goes through a discrete cosine transform and is quantised with the standard JPEG tables, then decoded again. Re-saves shift the block grid so generation loss compounds like reposting a meme.

video

Video gets extras stills can't: dropped frame rates, handheld shake, tape tracking errors, chroma bleed, interlacing, ghosting trails, exposure flicker, moving light leaks, animated grain and a date stamp whose clock ticks. Exports record in real time to MP4 (or WebM in browsers without MP4 recording), and the bitrate slider lets the video codec add its own smear.

private by design

Nothing is uploaded. There's no account, no tracking and no server processing. Your photos and videos are read, edited and saved entirely on your device.

faq
which browsers work?
Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, Android and iOS. Video export needs MediaRecorder: Chrome, Edge and Safari save MP4, Firefox saves WebM.
why does export take as long as the video?
Recording happens while the clip plays at normal speed, so audio stays in sync. Keep the tab visible until it finishes.
can I share a look?
Yes. "copy look link" puts every setting into the URL, so anyone opening it gets the same look.
what does bleach bypass do?
It recreates the film-lab process of skipping the bleach stage: luminance is laid back over the colour, so contrast goes up, saturation drops and highlights turn silvery.
