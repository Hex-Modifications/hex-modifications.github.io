Map of Leonida

Leonida is Rockstar's Florida, so this map uses real Florida geography with every official region and 533 landmarks mapped by the GTA VI community. Search for a place or tap any dot.

    Leonida

    Pick a place

    Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. Big labels are Rockstar's six official regions; pink pins are named places from Rockstar's profiles and artwork; small dots are landmarks mapped by the community, with the real building each one is based on.

    Rockstar hasn't released the in-game map yet, so positions follow the real-life locations. Landmark data: gtadb.org (MIT). Geography: Natural Earth.
