Everything we know

Vice City, USA.

Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they end up on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida.

“If anything happens, I'm right behind you.”

Jason Duval

Another day in paradise, right?

One half of the lead duo. Raised around con artists and criminals, Jason joined the Army to escape a rough adolescence, then drifted down to the Keys and back into what he knows: running errands for local drug smugglers.

“Only smart moves from here.”

Lucia Caminos

The only thing that matters is who you know and what you got.

The other lead. Taught to fight by her father from childhood, Lucia ended up in the Leonida Penitentiary after standing up for her family, and walked out by sheer luck. Now she is done with half-measures and wants the better life her mother dreamed of back in Liberty City.

Quick answers

All questions →
When does GTA 6 come out?

Grand Theft Auto VI releases on November 19, 2026.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's site says it plays best on PlayStation 5.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date yet. Previous GTA games reached PC after their console launch.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US it's reported at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Two protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
