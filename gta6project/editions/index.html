Confirmed

Standard Edition

$79.99 (US) Reported price

The base game. Pre-orders made before launch include the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Source: PlayStation Store listing as reported by Game Informer and others

Confirmed

Ultimate Edition

$99.99 (US) Reported price

The base game plus a collection of exclusive vehicles, weapons, shops and outfits woven through Jason and Lucia's story. Reportedly digital only.

  • '95 Grotti Cheetah
  • Squalo speedboat
  • Vapid off-road buggy
  • Vapid Ganado retro build
  • "One-Eyed Willie" Canis off-roader
  • Wyman Car Collection
  • Safehouse vehicles
  • Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers
  • Engraved sidearm variants
  • Electric Fang Tattoo
  • Rideout Customs
  • Sara's Salon
  • Stock 305
  • PTT store
  • Goodtime Gear apparel
  • Vice City Style outfits

Source: Rockstar Games official media pack (Ultimate Edition Benefits) · Rockstar's Ultimate Edition page ↗

Confirmed

Vintage Vice City Pack

Free pre-order bonus

A retro bonus that harks back to Vice City's neon heyday: a '55 Vapid Stanier, exclusive 80s-style outfits and hairstyles, and a vintage weapon pattern. Reportedly free with every pre-order and every digital purchase made before November 20, 2026.

  • '55 Vapid Stanier
  • Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach (reported)
  • Exclusive vintage looks and hairstyles
  • Vintage Vice City weapon pattern

Source: Rockstar Games official site and media pack (Vintage Vice City Pack)

Confirmed

The Vice City Collection

Collector's box · Rockstar Store

A collector's box of physical merchandise sold through the Rockstar Store, separate from the game editions.

Source: Rockstar Newswire, 8 October 2026
