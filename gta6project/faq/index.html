GTA VI · Questions

FAQ

Short, sourced answers. If Rockstar hasn't confirmed something, we say so.

When does GTA 6 come out?

Grand Theft Auto VI releases on November 19, 2026.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's site says it plays best on PlayStation 5.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date yet. Previous GTA games reached PC after their console launch.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US it's reported at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Two protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In Vice City and the wider state of Leonida, inspired by modern-day Florida. Rockstar has named six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

What is the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier, exclusive vintage outfits and hairstyles, and a weapon pattern.

What's in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

Exclusive vehicles (including the '95 Grotti Cheetah and the Squalo speedboat), weapons such as the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, shops including Electric Fang Tattoo and Rideout Customs, and outfits.

Who voices Jason and Lucia?

Rockstar hasn't announced the cast. Stephen Root has said he plays Brian Heder; fans believe Lucia is voiced by Manni L. Perez and Jason by Dylan Rourke, but neither is confirmed.

Who made the GTA 6 soundtrack?

The original score is by Justice (out December 11), and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 tracks) releases November 19 with artists including Travis Scott, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen and Rauw Alejandro.

Is this site official?

No. Leonida Files is an unofficial fan database by Hex Modifications, and isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

What leaked from GTA 6?

Two big leaks: around 90 clips of early footage in September 2022 after a hack, and the "CyberLeek" videos from a near-final build in August 2026. Our Leaks & rumours page describes what they showed, with links to the coverage.
