GTA6 Project · Verified sellers

Selling mods

Most mods on GTA6 Project are free. A small group of verified sellers, approved by hand, can also list premium mods. Buyers always pay through a storefront Rockstar has approved, so creators get paid and stay inside Rockstar's rules.

Selling opens with uploads

Selling opens after GTA VI reaches PC and uploads go live. Founding creators can register interest now.

How to become a verified seller

  1. Earn your placeHave at least five approved free mods on GTA6 Project, six months of history and a clean record: no rule breaks, no stolen assets, no unresolved reports.
  2. ApplySend your profile, your best work and what you plan to sell. We look at quality, support history and how you treat users.
  3. Manual approvalA staff member reviews every application by hand. There's no automatic route and no paying to skip the queue.
  4. Set up on an approved storeSell through a storefront Rockstar has approved, such as the Cfx Marketplace once it covers GTA VI. We check your store listing matches your upload.
  5. List on GTA6 ProjectYour premium mod gets a full page here, with screenshots, reviews, the price and a buy button that goes to your approved store listing.

Rules for paid mods

01

Approved store only

Every sale goes through a storefront Rockstar has approved. No PayPal links, Patreon paywalls, Discord sales or direct bank transfers.

02

Original work

Everything in a paid mod must be yours or licensed for commercial use. No Rockstar assets, missions or DLC behind a paywall, and no real brands.

03

Keep releasing free work

Paid mods sit beside free work, not instead of it. Sellers keep publishing free mods too.

04

No gambling or chance

No loot boxes, gacha, random rewards for money, in-game currency sales, crypto or NFTs.

05

Honest listings

Real in-game screenshots, clear features, the game version it works on, and a refund policy that follows UK and EU consumer law.

06

Support and updates

Paying users get fixes when a game update breaks the mod. Abandoned paid mods are delisted.

07

Same review as free mods

Paid mods go through the full human review and virus scan, and can be pulled at any time if they break the rules.

Losing seller status: Selling status is removed for stolen or ripped content, selling outside approved stores, misleading listings, ignoring refunds or support, or any breach of Rockstar's guidelines.

Why sales go through approved stores

Rockstar's modding guidelines say mods can only be sold through storefronts it has approved, and that modding everywhere else has to be non-commercial. Rockstar's own Cfx Marketplace is the first of these. Sending buyers there keeps your mod, and this site, on the right side of Rockstar. If Rockstar approves GTA6 Project as a storefront, verified sellers will be able to sell directly on the site. Until then, every purchase happens on an approved store.
