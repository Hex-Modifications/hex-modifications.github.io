GTA6 Project · Rules

Upload rules

Two layers: Rockstar's own modding guidelines, which every mod must follow, and our quality bar on top.

Rockstar's guidelines

Rockstar published updated modding guidelines on 10 September 2026, covering GTA VI as well as GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. In short:

Respect the storyDon't expand or change Rockstar's official storylines, missions, characters or continuity.
No cross-game portsDon't bring maps, locations, characters or assets from one Rockstar game into another.
No unofficial portsDon't port games to platforms Rockstar hasn't released them on.
Multiplayer through Rockstar onlyMultiplayer modding goes through Rockstar's licensed Creator Platform (FiveM/RedM), unless Rockstar says otherwise.
No other people's IPNo real brands, logos, car makes, music, artwork or other games' content, and no Rockstar voice performances reused.
No real people without permissionNo real person's voice, image or likeness without written permission.
Paid mods through approved stores onlyMods can only be sold through storefronts Rockstar approves, such as its Cfx Marketplace. Everywhere else, modding has to be non-commercial.
No gambling, loot boxes or cryptoNo real-money gambling, loot boxes, sale of in-game currency, or crypto, NFTs or memecoins.

Summarised from Game Developer ↗ · GamesRadar ↗ · Rockstar mod guidelines ↗ · TechRadar: Cfx Marketplace ↗. Rockstar's own wording always wins. This isn't legal advice.

Our quality bar

01

Rules first

Every upload must follow Rockstar's modding guidelines and our rules. Anything that breaks them is rejected, however good it is.

02

Your own work

Original work, or work you have written permission to use, with every contributor credited. No ripped or stolen assets.

03

Single-player only

Nothing that works online, gives an unfair advantage or touches GTA Online.

04

Proper presentation

At least three unedited in-game screenshots, a clear description, install and uninstall steps, requirements and a version number.

05

Tested and stable

Tested on the current game version, with no crashes, no broken saves and no major performance hit.

06

Clean and safe

Every file is virus-scanned. Script mods need readable code or pass a manual code review; no obfuscated installers or bundled adware.

07

Lore-friendly

Content that looks like it belongs in Leonida: fictional brands, original designs, Rockstar's tone.

08

Maintained

A changelog for every update, and a fix or a takedown when a game update breaks it.

Review process

  1. SubmitUpload your files, screenshots and description from your creator account.
  2. Automated checksVirus scan, file-type and size checks, and a duplicate check against existing uploads.
  3. Human reviewA moderator installs and tests the mod against the rules and the quality bar.
  4. PublishedApproved mods go live with your name on them. Great work gets a Staff Pick.

Takedowns and reports

Rights holders and creators can report a mod at hexmodifications@icloud.com. We remove infringing or unsafe files quickly and tell the uploader why.

Founding creators

Get in before PC launch

Creators who join before GTA VI reaches PC get a permanent Founding Creator badge, your name reserved, and first access to uploads. Accounts open soon; until then, register by email.

  • Creator: Anyone with a verified account who has had a mod approved.
  • Verified creator: Three or more approved mods with no rule breaks. Faster reviews and a badge.
  • Verified seller: Manually approved creators with a track record who can list paid mods. Application only.
  • Founding creator: Creators who join before the PC launch. A permanent badge and early access to uploads.
