Mods open when GTA VI comes to PC

GTA VI launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version, and consoles don't run mods, so there's nothing to upload yet. We're building the hub, the rules and the developer guides now, so creators are ready on day one.

Founding creators

Get in before PC launch

Creators who join before GTA VI reaches PC get a permanent Founding Creator badge, your name reserved, and first access to uploads. Accounts open soon; until then, register by email.

  • Creator: Anyone with a verified account who has had a mod approved.
  • Verified creator: Three or more approved mods with no rule breaks. Faster reviews and a badge.
  • Verified seller: Manually approved creators with a track record who can list paid mods. Application only.
  • Founding creator: Creators who join before the PC launch. A permanent badge and early access to uploads.
