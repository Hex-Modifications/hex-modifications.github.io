Using the brand

Do

Keep the 6 pink

"GTA" is white, the 6 is Flamingo pink, and the pill sits to the right of the wordmark or under the icon.

Do

Credit Hex Modifications

When space allows, add "by Hex Modifications" or use the lockup. GTA6 Project and Hex Modifications are separate sites from the same studio.

Don't

Imply it's official

Never pair our logo with Rockstar's logo or say GTA6 Project is endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

Don't

Redraw or recolour it

No stretching, outlines, new colours or effects. Use the files above on dark backgrounds.

Questions or a different format? Email hexmodifications@icloud.com.
