Leaks & rumours

GTA VI has had two major leaks and years of rumours. Here's what was reported, described in our own words with links to the original coverage. We don't host leaked files: the sources below covered them at the time.

18 September 2022

The 2022 hack

Rumour

Around 90 clips (about an hour) of early development footage were posted to GTAForums by a user calling themselves "teapotuberhacker", after an attacker phished their way into Rockstar's internal Slack. A UK teenager linked to the Lapsus$ group was later found responsible by a UK court. Rockstar's Aaron Garbut later told Bloomberg it felt "deeply invasive".

What it showed

  • A Vice City setting, with "Vice City" on signs and buildings in a modern-day city
  • Two protagonists, a woman and her male partner (later revealed as Lucia and Jason)
  • A diner robbery at "Hank's Waffles", which Rockstar later showed polished in the Extended Look
  • A Vice City metro, with Jason getting on and off a train
  • Explorable interiors, carrying bodies and a lower crouch
  • A redesigned weapon wheel and GTA V-style gunplay
  • Police who reload in real time during shootouts
  • The Sanchez and Sultan among the vehicles

15 August 2026

The CyberLeek videos

Rumour

From mid-August 2026 an anonymous leaker calling themselves "CyberLeek" posted short clips from an apparently playable build, tied to a memecoin that let fans pay to vote on the next leak. Reports put the total at 16+ clips and around 30 minutes by August 26, plus a full map image; a later batch reportedly included mission cutscenes. IGN reported the leaker appeared to cash out about $270,000. Rockstar called it heartbreaking for the team and confirmed the November 19 date.

What it showed

  • Jason playing basketball, driving and fighting pedestrians
  • An image said to show the full map
  • A later 25-minute batch said to include missions and cutscenes (authenticity unverified)
  • No arrest had been reported as of late August 2026

How GTA VI was made

GTA Intel recap of Bloomberg's feature ↗
  1. 2015

    Project Americas

    Rockstar told Bloomberg the game began life as "Project Americas", meant to span large parts of North and South America. Senior staff flew to Miami that year for the first research trip.

  2. 2016–2018

    On hold for Red Dead

    GTA VI was first meant to be made alongside Red Dead Redemption 2, but as Red Dead grew, most of the studio moved onto finishing it and GTA VI slipped back years.

  3. 2019

    The story takes shape

    By the end of 2019 the leads had the skeleton of the story: Jason and Lucia, a criminal couple robbing their way through a fictional Miami. The scope was later cut back to Miami and its surroundings.

  4. 2020–2021

    Pandemic slowdown

    Covid shut Rockstar's performance-capture studios, hiring made communication harder and some senior designers quit. Rob Nelson called it a dark period.

  5. 2022

    A playable whole

    By 2022, Bloomberg wrote, there were finally enough working pieces to see the whole game.

  6. 2024

    Back to the office

    Rockstar required staff in the office five days a week, partly over security.

From Bloomberg Businessweek's October 2026 feature, based on interviews with Rockstar's leadership.
