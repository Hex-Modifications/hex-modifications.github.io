GTA VI · Gameplay

How it plays

What Rockstar has actually shown of GTA VI in action, mostly from Rockstar's Extended Look (August 27, 2026) and the accompanying Newswire post.

01

Two protagonists

Swap between Jason and Lucia with a selection wheel (solo Jason, solo Lucia, or both together), with an instant swap when they're side by side.

Confirmed
02

Six-star wanted level

Shaking a six-star wanted level can mean ditching or torching the car and switching vehicles.

Confirmed
03

Stealing cars

Two ways to take a car: a loud physical struggle, or quietly with a slim jim.

Confirmed
04

Handling tuned by racers

Rockstar says real race drivers helped tune the driving; upgrades change performance and handling, not just looks.

Confirmed
05

Racing

Circuit racing, dirt-bike and off-road events were shown.

Confirmed
06

Vehicle combat

Shooting out police tyres and firing at pursuing helicopters.

Confirmed
07

Land, sea and air

Airboats, jet skis, kayaks, e-scooters, snorkelling and scuba diving, and skydiving over Vice City.

Confirmed
08

Convertible roofs

A convertible lowering its roof on the move anchors the "go for a drive" set-piece.

Confirmed
09

Single-player

Rockstar's pre-order materials describe a single-player experience; no online mode has been announced.

Reported
10

Social feeds

The Real Dimez's rise plays out on in-game social media feeds.

Reported
